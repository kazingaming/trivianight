64. Four people must cross a bridge at night. Their crossing times are 1, 2, 7, and 10 minutes. Only two can cross at once, they need one flashlight, and a pair moves at the slower person's speed. What is the fastest possible time for everyone to cross?
Answer: 17 minutes
Type: Optimization / Logic
 Category: Logic
 Difficulty: Hard
 Reveal: The trick is making the two slowest cross together:
1 & 2 cross = 2
1 returns = 1
7 & 10 cross = 10
2 returns = 2
1 & 2 cross = 2
Total: 17 minutes.

73. A factory makes widgets, and 1% are defective. A scanner correctly flags 99% of defective widgets, but also falsely flags 5% of good widgets. If a widget gets flagged, roughly what is the chance it is actually defective?
Answer: About 17%
Type: Probability / Base-rate intuition
 Category: Logic / Probability
 Difficulty: Hard
 Reveal: Imagine 10,000 widgets:
100 are defective → about 99 flagged correctly
9,900 are good → about 495 falsely flagged
So there are about 594 flagged widgets, but only 99 are genuinely defective:
99 ÷ 594 ≈ 16.7%
A highly accurate test can still produce mostly false alarms when the thing being tested for is rare.
